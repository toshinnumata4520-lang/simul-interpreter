Simultaneous Interpreter (Japanese ⇄ English)

This app translates speech on the spot and shows it as large subtitles. Use Safari on iPhone, or Chrome on Android and computers (Edge also works on computers). The app shows English automatically if your phone is set to English. You can also change it in Settings → Display language (表示言語).

Scan this QR code with your phone's camera
https://toshinnumata4520-lang.github.io/simul-interpreter/

First-time setup (1–2 minutes, once only)

  1. Open the app from the QR code. The Settings screen appears.
  2. Tick the box "I will not use this for meetings, parent conversations, or students' personal information".
  3. Tap Easy setup with Google and choose your Google account.
  4. If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", tap Advanced → Go to 同時通訳 かんたん設定 (unsafe).
    This appears because the app was built for internal use at our school.
  5. On the permission screen, tap Continue (or Allow).
  6. Wait 30–60 seconds until you see ✓ Setup complete. Tap Done to close Settings.

If it doesn't work, open If it doesn't work (manual setup) on the Settings screen and follow those steps instead.

How to use

  1. Tap Start at the bottom of the screen and allow microphone access.
  2. Speak, and the translation appears in large text.
  3. Use the bottom-left button to choose the direction: JA→EN / EN→JA / Auto (detects which language is spoken).
  4. A− / A＋ changes the text size. Orig shows or hides the original speech.
  5. Tap Stop when you finish. Turning off the screen or switching apps also stops it.
⚠ Important
This app uses the free tier of your own Google account. Conversations sent on the free tier may be used by Google to improve its services, and may be read by people.
Do not use it for meetings, conversations with parents, or anything involving students' names, grades, or family circumstances.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?
No. It runs on the free tier of your own Google account (about 40–50 minutes of continuous speech per day).

Do I share the limit with other staff?
No. Everyone uses their own free allowance.

On Android, it beeps and cuts off every few seconds
That's how Chrome's speech recognition works on Android. If it bothers you, set the direction button to Auto.

How do I stop using it?
Clear your browser's history and site data to remove the settings from your device.
